Equity index source review — 2026-10-18

Required: free access, commercial forecasting, public chart/API redistribution,
and for genuine hourly forecasting, historical intraday observations.
Outcome: TAIEX daily history is eligible for commercial reuse and public display
with attribution; no hourly benchmark-index feed was verified.
No unverified equity data has been added to production. Free API access, an SDK
licence, a government portal listing, or attribution alone does not grant
third-party index redistribution rights.

0. APPROVED: TAIEX daily historical open dataset
https://data.gov.tw/en/datasets/11755
https://data.gov.tw/license
https://www.twse.com.tw/en/terms/use.html
Dataset 11755 explicitly designates free Open Government Data License v1.0.
The licence grants royalty-free reproduction, distribution, public transmission
and adaptation for any purpose, subject to the stated attribution. TWSE's own
website terms section 8 expressly excepts materials authorised via Government
Open Data Platform. The specific linked CSV resource, not unrelated website
content, is used:
https://www.twse.com.tw/indicesReport/MI_5MINS_HIST?response=open_data
Official API schema corroborates the daily fields Date and ClosingIndex:
https://openapi.twse.com.tw/v1/swagger.json
Resource is daily OHLC despite the historical endpoint name containing 5MINS.
This approval is for the open daily dataset, not paid intraday/delayed feeds,
index-linked financial products, trademark rights or provider endorsement.
Attribution names TWSE/FSC, dataset title/ID, 2026, licence/version and URL;
forecasts are explicitly our derived output. Historical month responses are
cached with integrity hashes; ongoing runs retrieve the current month only.

1. Financial Services Commission / Korea open-data portal, index quotations
https://www.data.go.kr/data/15094807/openapi.do
https://www.data.go.kr/catalog/15094807/openapi.json
Current dataset metadata (updated 2026-09-07) specifies KOGL type 4:
attribution, non-commercial, no derivatives. Description also prohibits
unauthorised third-party provision/redistribution and directs commercial users
to KRX. Excluded. The general FSC FAQ explicitly makes rights dataset-specific.
https://www.fsc.go.kr/in060501

2. FRED / S&P 500
https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/SP500
https://www.stlouisfed.org/publications/page-one-economics/2021/10/15/ethical-use-of-data-with-fred
S&P series is copyrighted third-party data, daily close. FRED says commercial
redistribution of copyrighted series requires the owner's authorisation.
Excluded from public production without that grant.

3. Nasdaq Global Index Data Service
https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/TraderNews.aspx?id=DN2024-5
Notice effective August 2024 requires direct licensing for feed recipients
of end-of-day/historical data and prior approval for redistribution. The old
claim that post-18:30 index data needs no licence is expressly superseded.
Excluded without a compatible licence.

4. Twelve Data
https://twelvedata.com/terms
https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/5332349-commercial-and-personal-usage
Free-tier commercial use is prohibited in the terms. External display and
redistribution depend on plan, exchange permissions and agreements. Excluded.

5. Tiingo IEX
https://www.tiingo.com/products/iex-api
Free/internal and paid display-redistribution tiers are separate. The product
is equity quotes, not a free licence for the named benchmark indices. Excluded.

6. OHLC
https://ohlc.io/pricing
Free tier is personal use; commercial display and redistribution are separate
paid tiers. It does not supply a free benchmark-index display grant. Excluded.

7. Convex
https://convextrade.com/open-data
https://convextrade.com/terms
Open-data page explicitly permits attribution-based commercial reuse of its
own CONVEX_* computed series (CC BY 4.0), but source data retains original
provider licences. Its proprietary macro composites are not KOSPI/S&P/Nasdaq
stock indices; listed updates are daily or slower. General terms also restrict
commercial redistribution absent authorisation. Not a verified replacement.

8. OECD share prices
https://www.oecd.org/en/data/indicators/share-prices.html
https://www.oecd.org/en/about/terms-conditions.html
Published indicator is monthly averages, not hourly quotes. OECD terms permit
commercial data reuse except where third-party/additional restrictions apply
and require checking source metadata. No blanket pass-through grant for the
underlying exchange index has been established. Not connected as intraday data.

9. Dukascopy XML
https://www.dukascopy.com/plugins/cont.php?ref_id=1273
Specific XML licence is non-commercial and restricts redistribution. It cannot
serve as a commercial-public grant for index quotes or other feed products.

Stooq official pages https://stooq.com/db/h/ and https://stooq.com/t/ were not
readable through this research tool, so no positive reuse conclusion was made.

Implemented: TAIEX daily-source adapter and opt-in hourly issuance of daily-target forecasts.
This preserves daily source resolution and immutable historical issues. It is
not an hourly market-price model. A compatible data licence/source remains
necessary to activate KOSPI/S&P/Nasdaq or genuine intraday forecasts.
