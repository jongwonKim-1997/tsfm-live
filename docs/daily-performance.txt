Daily public performance tables

The production board remains daily, as requested on 2026-10-18. Hourly runs
refresh actuals and publication; they do not imply hourly observation targets.

The home page contains separate indicator/model performance and model ranking
tables. They derive exclusively from genuinely issued daily forecasts and
subsequently observed actuals in the public daily payload. No backtest or
research benchmark records are substituted for live issued records.

Evaluation window: available actuals overlapping the last 30 days of issues.
Each model and target date counts once, using its first valid issue. Issuance
must precede the target close; its anchor must have closed by issuance. The
actual and target close timestamps must match, and the actual must have closed
by both the snapshot generation time and display time. Future, invalid, or
unsettled records do not count. Revised actuals can change measured performance.

Indicator metrics:
- Number of distinct settled targets.
- Direction hit rate from the last input value, with hits and denominator.
  Unchanged actuals are excluded; unchanged forecasts on moving actuals miss.
- Mean absolute error in the indicator's unit (percentage points for yields).
- Actual coverage of nominal 80% prediction intervals.
No metric is presented as a calibrated probability of future success.

Rankings use only common model/target cases with the same issue day and anchor.
The anchor value, anchor date and anchor close must match. Each indicator's
score is 1 - sum(model absolute error) / sum(persistence baseline error).
The baseline holds the last input value constant. Overall scores give every
indicator equal weight. Higher is better; negative means worse than baseline.
The baseline denominator must be positive. Every indicator must have at least
20 paired targets before ranks are displayed. Ties share a rank. These are
provisional descriptive rankings, not claims of statistical superiority.
If any indicator cannot be scored, the overall score is absent. Below the rank
threshold, an available score can be shown but no ordinal rank is assigned.

On launch (2026-10-18), all forecasts target unreleased future dates. Tables
therefore correctly show zero evaluated cases and no success rates or ranks.
As the daily public payload refreshes, settled results populate automatically.
